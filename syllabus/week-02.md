# Week 2 — Data structures, pointers and code organisation

**Daily session:** 30 minutes (5 min recall, 10 min study, 15 min coding).

## Daily targets

1. **19 Nov — Arrays and slices**
   - Target: Store service names; practise len, cap, append and range.
   - Study: https://go.dev/tour/moretypes/7

2. **20 Nov — Maps**
   - Target: Model service status and hostname latency; practise comma-ok.
   - Study: https://go.dev/tour/moretypes/19

3. **21 Nov — Structs**
   - Target: Define Host, CheckResult and Service structs.
   - Study: https://go.dev/tour/moretypes/1

4. **22 Nov — Pointers**
   - Target: Update a Host through a pointer.
   - Study: https://go.dev/tour/moretypes/1

5. **23 Nov — Methods and receivers**
   - Target: Add methods; compare value and pointer receivers.
   - Study: https://go.dev/tour/methods/1

6. **24 Nov — Packages and modules**
   - Target: Use go mod init; split internal/checks and internal/report.
   - Study: https://go.dev/doc/tutorial/create-module

7. **25 Nov — Weekly review: host inventory**
   - Target: Combine structs, slices, maps, methods and at least two packages.
   - Study: https://gobyexample.com/structs

## Weekly outcome
Model data with structs, slices, maps, methods and packages.
